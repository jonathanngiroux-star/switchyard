# Pricing

Public page. No "contact sales" below Enterprise — self-serve with a card.

| Tier | Price | Seats | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-host core | **Free** (MIT) | unlimited | one binary + SQLite; you run it, you own it |
| Cloud Starter | **$29/mo** | 10 | hosted, backups, upgrades, email support |
| Cloud Pro | **$500/mo** | 50 | + VPC peering, SSO/SAML, audit logs |
| Enterprise | **$1,500/mo** | unlimited | + air-gap install, SLA, DPA, SCIM |

Cloud is operational risk transfer: we page, back up, and upgrade. It is not flags-ripped-from-core — the self-hosted evaluator stays complete.

## What will never be paywalled

Evaluation. Flags must evaluate locally in your services, self-hosted, forever. Gating evaluation behind cloud is an investor "no" and a product sin.
